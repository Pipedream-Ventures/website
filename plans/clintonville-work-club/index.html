The actual decision

Three ways
forward.

This is not a pitch for one predetermined answer. It is a proposal to investigate three paths together and choose the one that gives Morgan the best mix of freedom, creativity, income, and joy.

01 / Buy

Acquire a great Columbus business.

Use SBA financing to buy proven cash flow, replace Morgan's salary, and improve a company that already has customers and operating history.

Best for: financial safety and a faster exit from her job
02 / Build

Create Clintonville Work Club.

Build the neighborhood place we wish existed, with Morgan's interiors, furniture, fashion, vintage, and hospitality instincts at the center.

Best for: creative ownership and long-term upside
03 / Sequence

Buy the engine, then fund the dream.

Acquire a strong operating business first, stabilize it with management, then use the cash flow and experience to launch the Work Club with less pressure.

Best for: getting both without betting everything at once

The experience

One club.

The concept works when each use has a clear home. Members should never have to choose between a productive room and a fun room.

01 / Focus

The Library

A genuinely silent room. Deep work, writing, reading, and no calls. The promise members can trust every day.

02 / Connect

The Café

Great coffee, communal tables, soft seating, natural conversation, and the everyday hum of people making things.

03 / Unwind

The Bar

A separate, lockable lounge that opens after work. Wine, beer, a tight cocktail list, and small plates without nightclub energy.

04 / Discover

Morgan's Shop

Fashion, vintage, objects, and original furniture through Morgan's eye. Curated drops and commissions, not racks of random inventory.

The bar question

Yes, it can
be attached.

The bar should feel connected socially and separate operationally. The 24/7 club cannot become an unattended liquor premises.

One front door.
Two controlled worlds.

Members move naturally from work to happy hour, while lockable doors, controlled alcohol storage, sound separation, and defined hours keep the operation clean.

The proposal

Let's investigate this together, then choose.

  1. Talk through what Morgan actually wants her days to feel like: designing, building, styling, managing people, serving customers, or some mix.
  2. Call the brokers for 4784 and 2840. Confirm availability, full economics, permitted uses, delivery condition, parking rights, patio rights, and exclusivity.
  3. Have an Ohio liquor attorney perform a parcel-specific permit and premises check before relying on bar revenue.
  4. Interview three SBA lenders: one strong 7(a) acquisition lender, one startup-friendly lender, and one local 504 CDC.
  5. Launch a beautiful founding-member page with a refundable $100 deposit, a clear founding price, and Morgan's creative point of view at the center.
  6. Tour the serious properties only after the first demand signal, then price the actual build with a contractor.
  7. Request financials on the best Columbus acquisitions and compare post-debt owner income, workload, creative fit, and downside against the Work Club.
  8. Choose the dream, the cash engine, or the sequence that gets us both. No forcing it because a listing happens to exist.
