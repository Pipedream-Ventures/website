App Store

1Growth

Competitors are bidding on your name

A Bible Captions ad sits above you for your own brand search. I'd defend the brand on Apple Ads, especially if organic or social is sending people to search for you. We deal with this at Rapchat. It sucks to pay for it, but it's usually worth it.

2Store

Put Johnny front and center

Johnny could be way more prominent in the screenshots, and they could use a polish. He might even belong on the app icon.

Store

The old app is still live

"Old app - Core of the Heart" (Fourthwall, 788 ratings) is still for sale and shows up under "You Might Also Like" on your new listing. Once migration is done I'd pull it from sale, and until then make sure it points people to the new app.

Small layout bug on the quote

UX

The survey feels a bit laggy

Some form fields and the onboarding survey are a tad laggy, and the date picker doesn't look like iOS. Not a big deal, just noticed. What's the tech stack? Is it native?

UX

Catch the people who skip the video

If someone skips the intro video before 30 seconds, the next few screens could still introduce Johnny in a quick slideshow.

Bug

Stuck on enable notifications

I got stuck on this step and couldn't tell whether notifications actually got enabled. Analytics would show how many people hit this.

4UX

Nav bars jump around

The top nav sometimes sits lower or in a different spot between screens. Probably the component or tech stack. Worth making it consistent.

5UX

Comments are sticky but in a weird spot

Comments look super popular, but they live in the floating bar with a red badge count that pulls attention while you're reading. I'd find a calmer home that still shows they're active.

Question

Johnny or Gospel Bot?

Not sure how much Gospel Bot gets used. If it's light, that tab might be better spent on Johnny himself.

Pricing

Six plans, and one costs more on annual

PlanMonthly12 × monthlyAnnualAnnual vs monthly
Silver$33$396$34912% less
Premium$44$528$59012% more
Gold$77$924$81911% less

Premium Annual is $62 more than paying monthly for a year. Anyone who does the math picks monthly. If Premium is a legacy tier, I'd retire it. If it's live on the paywall, I'd reprice it in line with the others.

Six SKUs is also a lot of choice, and "Premium" doesn't say where it sits between Silver and Gold. The plans are pricey in general, which may be by design. Conversion and retention by plan would tell us if it's working.

Source: in-app purchases listed on the US App Store page. I haven't confirmed which of these appear on the paywall.

To go deeper

What would help me next

Not a ton of big feedback here. Mostly small design and UX things, nothing crazy. To give more useful direction I'd want to know:

  1. The tech stack
  2. Who your users are and their full journey
  3. Analytics: funnel, retention, and which features are stickiest
  4. How much Gospel Bot actually gets used
  5. Which plans the paywall shows today
  6. Your rough roadmap

In general, a super fun and unique app. Congrats guys.
