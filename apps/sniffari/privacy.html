Sniffari Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Sniffari is made by Pipedream Ventures LLC. The short version: your walks, dogs, and photos stay on your iPhone. We don't run accounts, servers, ads, or analytics for Sniffari, and we don't collect or sell your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Walks: route, time, distance, pace, and any trims or notes.
  • Dogs and walkers: names, photos, breed, weight, age, and energy you enter.
  • Photos: pictures you pick or take for dog profiles and walks. Dog cutouts and character looks are made on your device.

This data is included in your iPhone backups and is deleted when you delete the app.

Permissions

  • Location draws the route and measures distance while a walk is running.
  • Notifications remind you if you stop for a while and may have forgotten to end the walk.
  • Apple Health (optional) saves walks as workouts and reads heart rate and past walks so you can import them. Health data is never used for advertising and never leaves your device through Sniffari.
  • Photos and Camera (optional) let you add dog photos and find pictures from a walk.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you tap Share or Save. Share cards and replays hide the start and end of the route by default. Once you share an image or video, the app you share it to handles it under its own policy.

Children

Sniffari is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact
