Walks and GPS

Walkies uses location only while a walk is running. If a route looks jumpy, keep the app allowed to use location "While Using" and walk with a clear view of the sky for the first minute. Forgot to end a walk? Open it in History and use Trim.

Sharing

Share cards and replays hide about 150 m at the start and end of each walk by default, so your home stays private. Turn off "Hide start and end" on the share screen if you want the whole route.

Your data

Walks, dogs, and photos are stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them. Walks you saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you remove them there.
