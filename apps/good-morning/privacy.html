Good Morning Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Good Morning is an alarm clock. After your alarm, it shows a short horoscope for your sign and one good-news story. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your name, birthday, sign, page theme, alarms and reminder settings are saved on your iPhone, and shared with the app's widgets on the same device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Horoscopes and good-news summaries ship inside the app. Reading them sends nothing anywhere.

Location and weather

If you allow location, the morning page shows today's weather. To get it, the app rounds your location to about 1 km and sends it to Open-Meteo, a free weather service. Nothing else goes with it: no name, no device ID. We don't store it. Open-Meteo's own terms are at open-meteo.com/en/terms.

If you don't allow location, the page has no weather and nothing is sent. You can change this any time in iOS Settings.

Good-news photos and links

Story photos load from the publisher's website (Good News Network, Good Good Good and The Optimist Daily). Like any website, their servers see your IP address when the photo loads. Tapping a story opens the publisher's page in your browser, under their own privacy policy.

Notifications

Alarms and reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. They don't pass through any server.

Children

Good Morning is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
