Everlight: Prayer Wall, Bible

Need help with Everlight, subscriptions, prayer wall content, scripture features, or account data? Contact the Everlight team and include the device you are using, your app version if available, and a short description of the issue.

Email: seth@ratiolabapps.com

We usually review support requests within a few business days.

Subscriptions

Everlight subscriptions are managed through Apple. To cancel or manage a subscription, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your Apple Account, then Subscriptions.

Prayer Wall

Prayer requests may be moderated before or after they appear in the app. Please do not include private, sensitive, emergency, or identifying information in a public prayer request.

Safety

Everlight offers prayer, scripture, and reflection support. It is not emergency support, medical care, therapy, legal advice, or clergy. If you may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted person now.
