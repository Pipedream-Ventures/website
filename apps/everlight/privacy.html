Fold Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Fold: Prayer Wall & Bible ("Fold," "we," "us," or "our") is operated by Ratio Labs, Inc. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Fold mobile app and related services.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Fold, we may collect:

  • Profile and preference data: display name, prayer reminders, saved settings, and membership or subscription status.
  • Prayer and scripture activity: prayer requests you submit, prayers you mark, saved passages, notes, reading activity, and guided prayer progress.
  • Companion messages: prompts and replies exchanged with the Fold companion so the service can respond and improve safety handling.
  • Purchase data: subscription product identifiers, entitlement status, transaction status, and App Store purchase metadata provided by Apple and our subscription tooling.
  • Technical data: app version, device type, operating system, crash diagnostics, request logs, and similar information used to operate and secure the service.

How We Use Information

We use this information to operate Fold, save your preferences, display prayer and scripture features, provide subscription access, moderate community content, improve reliability, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

Prayer Wall and Community Content

Prayer requests submitted to the Prayer Wall may be visible to other Fold users after moderation. Do not submit private, sensitive, or emergency information that you do not want other people to see. We may remove or limit content that appears unsafe, abusive, spammy, or unrelated to the app.

AI Companion and Safety

Fold may use third-party AI infrastructure to generate companion responses and scripture-related reflections. The companion is designed for encouragement and reflection. It is not God, clergy, therapy, medical care, legal advice, emergency services, or professional advice. If you may be in immediate danger, contact emergency services or a trusted person now.

Service Providers

We use service providers to operate Fold, including Apple for App Store purchases, Supabase for authentication and data storage, OpenRouter and its model providers for companion and moderation requests, Superwall for subscription access, and Firebase for analytics and crash reporting. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies and only as needed to provide their services to us.

Subscriptions and Payments

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment card information. We receive subscription and entitlement information needed to unlock paid features and support purchases.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide Fold, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and improve the service. Some diagnostic logs may be retained for a shorter period than account or content data.

Your Choices

You may stop using Fold at any time. You can delete your Fold profile and associated app data from Profile in the app. You can manage or cancel App Store subscriptions through your Apple account settings. You may also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to legal and operational limits.

Children

Fold is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will review the request.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the date above and post the revised policy on this page.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to seth@ratiolabapps.com.
