# AllTails

AllTails is a fun dog walking app for you and your pups. Its main job is to log
dog walks and let the people who walk the same dogs share a pack.

The website serves dog owners, family members accepting an invite, and dog
walkers joining clients' packs. A visitor should be able to install the beta,
record a first walk, find a past walk, or recover when something goes wrong.

The October 19, 2026 website request is to explain beta access, provide a
beautiful user manual, and add a help center. Support House is a future step.
Current help goes to the existing support email and TestFlight feedback.

Product facts and labels come from the AllTails app, not design references.
`README.md` lists the source files and checked app revision. Voice and visual
authority remain the native repository's `COPY.md` and `DESIGN.md`.

This site is static. Help search filters local articles. It does not send
queries or open a support ticket. Packs, accounts, Health, and walk recording
are app features, not website features. The website must not simulate them.

As of this content update, the public install path is TestFlight. App Store
submission and the public-download switch have separate release approval.

Reading interfaces use a single content column, per Seth's October 19 feedback.
Use the sticky Contents menu for wayfinding instead of a sidebar.
