Other checks

Court records and Reddit

Tuscarawas CourtView returned 11 name matches for “Groovfold” across its listed sites. They date from 1986 to 2012: five workers' compensation cases, five records where Groovfold is listed as an employer in family matters, and one 1993 civil complaint (case 93CV020043). All are marked closed. The same business-name search returned no records for “All Wood Lockers” or “Cornell Properties.” These are local index results, not a search of every court.

The Ohio AG consumer-lawsuit index returned zero Groovfold results, as did a search for All Wood Lockers. Reddit searches for Groovfold and All Wood Lockers found no firsthand complaint under either name. Those searches do not rule out cases or posts filed under other names, in other places, or outside the indexed systems.

I have not seen the reported customer's quote, payment record, promised ship date, or Groovfold's response. Those documents are needed to explain that order.
