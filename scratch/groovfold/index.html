01 / The short read

Groovfold is an identifiable Ohio manufacturer, and All Wood Lockers is its brand. Public records show business activity into 2026 and a factory lease written through 2036. Those records do not prove a specific order was fulfilled, rent is current, or the company's financial health.

02 / Company

A real operation, with recent public signals.

All Wood Lockers describes itself as a Groovfold brand at 1050 W State St, Newcomerstown, Ohio. Groovfold's company history dates its founding to 1963. Recent public records show quotes, approvals, payments, and local participation; none independently verifies the status of a particular customer's order.

  • JAN 2026

    Public-school quote approved. New Philadelphia Board of Education minutes approve a $5,400 All Wood Lockers quote. Approval does not show delivery.

  • AUG 2026

    Local team listed. The Tuscarawas County YMCA names a Groovfold team at its golf outing.

  • 2024–25

    Other public buyers appear. John A. Logan College's packet includes a detailed locker quote; a Colorado school-district register records two payments.

The lease addendum recorded February 1, 2022 names Cornell Properties Ltd as landlord and Groovfold Inc. as tenant. It amends an October 2017 lease and adds a right of first refusal if the property is offered for sale. The auditor's transfer history shows Groovfold conveyed the property to Cornell Properties in 2017.

A county recorder name search showed no later indexed lease release through September 25, 2026. The written term and that search cannot prove present occupancy, rent payment, or exclude an unrecorded amendment or termination.

05 / Limits

What remains unverified.

The reported customer payment and delivery issue cannot be resolved from these public records. We have not verified the customer's contract, cleared payment, production status, shipment, refund, or the company's response. No conclusion about wrongdoing follows from this brief.

The Ohio Attorney General consumer-lawsuit index returned zero results for “Groovfold” and “All Wood Lockers” on September 28, 2026. Its scope is Ohio AG consumer-protection lawsuits since 2013; it does not clear private civil cases or other jurisdictions. The Tuscarawas County CourtView case search required reCAPTCHA, so no local name query was completed at publication.

06 / Next check
