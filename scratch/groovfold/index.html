What the records establish

  1. 01

    The company is registered

    The Ohio Secretary of State lists Groovfold, Inc. as active, entity 325132, first filed November 16, 1963. All Wood Lockers identifies Groovfold as its manufacturer.

  2. 02

    Lockers were at a customer site

    A June 2026 facilities work order for a gym renovation at the Howard T. Markey National Courts Building in Washington, DC says 15 court-provided All Wood Lockers units were already on site for a contractor to install. It does not say when they were purchased or address any other order.

  3. 03

    The company appeared in local activity

    New Philadelphia schools approved a $5,400 locker quote in January 2026. The Tuscarawas YMCA named a Groovfold team, including Andy Cornell and Grant Bennett, in August. Neither record proves the reported order’s status.

The reported 80-cabinet order

We were told 80 cabinets were supposed to be ready by Monday. We have not seen the written terms, a production update, or Groovfold’s response. We cannot tell from public records whether the cabinets were ready.

The next check is to have Groovfold confirm the order number, how many cabinets are complete, and the pickup or shipping plan through the phone number published on its site.

Research notes

Additional records and the limits of each check.

People and company structure

Groovfold says Richard Cornell founded it in 1963. A family obituary says his son Cris took over in 1977 and developed All Wood Lockers; Cris died in 2021. This describes the business history, not current ownership.

TuscLands identified Andy Cornell as Groovfold’s owner in 2025. The Ohio registry lists Julie Cornell as its active statutory agent since April 2021. A 2022 lease addendum calls her Groovfold’s president and sole shareholder at that time. These records do not resolve the current ownership split. The company site lists Grant Bennett as a sales contact. Kent State Tuscarawas lists Michael Cornell with Groovfold in a 2026 leadership class without stating his role.

Factory and lease

The county auditor lists Cornell Properties Ltd as owner of the Newcomerstown factory site. The recorded lease addendum names Groovfold as tenant from January 2022 through December 2036 at $8,000 per month. A recorder search found no later release through September 25, 2026. Current occupancy and payments are unverified.

Reviews and legal record searches

Birdeye mirrors three older positive Google reviews; none describes a locker order. The company’s own testimonials are not independent reviews. A BBB profile for related Wood Closet Designs shows zero complaints in its reporting window, generally three years.

Tuscarawas CourtView returned 11 Groovfold name matches dated 1986–2012, all marked closed. Five concerned workers’ compensation, five listed it as an employer in family matters, and one was a 1993 civil complaint (93CV020043). Its business-name search returned no result for All Wood Lockers or Cornell Properties. The Ohio AG consumer-lawsuit index returned no Groovfold result; All Wood Lockers also returned none. That index warns that cases filed before 2013 may be absent. Focused Groovfold and All Wood Lockers Reddit searches found no firsthand complaint. These searches do not cover every court, bankruptcy docket, or unindexed complaint. We have not reviewed the underlying 1993 civil complaint or completed a case-by-case check of the named people.
