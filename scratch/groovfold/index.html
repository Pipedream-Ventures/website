What the records establish

  1. 01

    The company is registered

    The Ohio Secretary of State lists Groovfold, Inc. as active, entity 325132, first filed November 16, 1963. All Wood Lockers identifies Groovfold as its manufacturer.

  2. 02

    Lockers were at a customer site

    A June 2026 facilities work order for a gym renovation at the Howard T. Markey National Courts Building in Washington, DC says 15 court-provided All Wood Lockers units were already on site for a contractor to install. It does not say when they were purchased or address any other order.

  3. 03

    Other buyers have documented locker projects

    New Philadelphia schools approved a $5,400 quote in January 2026. Public packets also show a $64,584 Johnson City bid and a $49,236 John A. Logan College quote. Approvals and quotes do not prove delivery.

  4. 04

    A 2022 SBA loan appears in federal data

    The SBA’s June 30, 2026 7(a) file lists a $300,000 Huntington SBA Express loan to Groovfold, approved June 8, 2022 and first disbursed July 6. Its status is “EXEMPT”; that file does not show whether payments are current.

The reported 80-cabinet order

We were told 80 cabinets were supposed to be ready Monday, September 28. Nobody has called the company for this brief. We have not seen a quote, written deadline, production update, or reply. About $30,000 came up earlier, but we have not seen a payment receipt or confirmed the payee. We cannot tell whether any cabinets are complete.

The next check is to ask Groovfold to confirm the order number, completed count, and pickup or shipping plan through the contact information published on its site.

  • Quote or order confirmation for the 80 cabinets
  • The written September 28 commitment
  • Payment receipt showing the payee, if money changed hands
  • Completed count and dated pickup or shipping confirmation

Research notes

Additional records and the limits of each check.

People, company, and contacts

Groovfold says Richard Cornell founded it in 1963. A family obituary says his son Cris took over in 1977 and developed All Wood Lockers; Cris died in 2021. The related BBB profile still names Cris as president, so that entry is stale.

TuscLands identified Andy Cornell as Groovfold’s owner in 2025. The Ohio registry lists Julie Cornell as statutory agent since April 2021 but does not list shareholders. A 2022 lease addendum called Julie Groovfold’s president and sole shareholder at that time and sole member of Cornell Properties Ltd. The records do not resolve present ownership. The company site names Grant Bennett as a sales contact. A 2026 YMCA golf writeup named Grant Bennett, Andrew Perez, Andy Cornell, and Chris Moran as a Groovfold group without job titles. Kent State Tuscarawas listed Michael Cornell with Groovfold in a 2026 leadership class without a title.

Groovfold, Inc. uses the All Wood Lockers, Wood Closet Designs, and All Wood Products names. The published business address is 1050 W State St, Newcomerstown, OH 43832. The locker site lists 800-367-1133, 740-498-8363, fax 740-498-8782, info@allwoodlockers.com, and grant@allwoodlockers.com; Groovfold also publishes andrew@groovfold.com.

Factory and lease

The county auditor lists Cornell Properties Ltd as owner of the 6.29-acre Newcomerstown site and shows 2025 property tax paid. Groovfold transferred the property to Cornell Properties in 2017 for a recorded $0 consideration. The recorded lease addendum names Groovfold as tenant from January 2022 through December 2036 at $8,000 per month. A recorder search found no later release through September 25, 2026. The index and tax status do not prove current occupancy or rent payments.

Loans and older plan filings

The SBA 7(a) file dated June 30, 2026 lists a $300,000 Huntington SBA Express loan with a $150,000 SBA guarantee, 6.25% variable rate, and 120-month term. The file marks it “EXEMPT,” a disbursed-loan status; it does not establish whether payments are current. The file gives a jobs number of two, which the SBA says it does not verify.

The SBA PPP file lists two Huntington loans under Groovfold’s name: $125,100 dated April 5, 2020, forgiven May 12, 2021 for $126,433.26; and $110,000 dated February 17, 2021, forgiven September 1, 2021 for $110,584.66. Both are marked paid in full. Their forms list 13 and 14 jobs respectively; those are borrower-reported counts, not a current headcount. A search of SBA files found no other 7(a) loan in the 2010–2019 file or 504 loan in the 2010–present file. Labor Department filings show Groovfold Inc. 401(k) Profit Sharing Plan years 2009–2012; the search found no later year. These searches are bounded by the named files and plan name.

Other public projects

The June 2026 federal work order says 15 All Wood Lockers units were at the Markey building for installation; it gives no purchase date. Public buyer records also show the January 2026 New Philadelphia school quote for $5,400, two May 2025 Weld County school payments of $1,125 and $4,317, a February 2025 Kendall County invoice for four sets of club lockers at $2,380, a December 2024 Johnson City bid for $64,584, a 2024 John A. Logan College quote for 69 lockers at $49,236, a January 2024 Lawrence County quote for $23,114, and a May 2022 Garden City Community College purchase recommendation for $19,262. The John A. Logan quote specified 7–10 weeks and, if a 50% deposit applied, the balance before shipment. Quotes, approvals, invoices, and payments show different stages; none speaks to this 80-cabinet order. The company site lists older installations, which are company claims.

Reviews, courts, and safety records

Birdeye mirrors three older positive Google reviews; none describes a locker order. The company’s own testimonials are not independent reviews. A BBB profile for related Wood Closet Designs shows zero complaints in its three-year reporting window.

Tuscarawas CourtView returned 11 Groovfold name matches dated 1986–2012, all marked closed: five workers’ compensation matters, five listing Groovfold as an employer, and one 1993 civil case, 93CV020043, in which Groovfold Fabricators was a defendant in Berry v. Pacemaker Plastics. Its business-name search returned no result for All Wood Lockers or Cornell Properties. A separate 1993 Eastern District of Pennsylvania bankruptcy adversary docket, 93-00712, lists Decora’s unsecured creditors committee against Groovfold; it was filed September 17, 1993 and closed March 11, 1994. The complaint itself was not available from the public docket site, so its substance is unknown.

The Ohio AG consumer-lawsuit index returned no Groovfold result; All Wood Lockers also returned none. Its coverage begins in 2013. A historical OSHA establishment search showed a serious citation issued July 20, 2000 at 1050 W State St, with a $5,100 penalty. Focused Groovfold and All Wood Lockers Reddit searches found no firsthand complaint. No result in these limited searches establishes that no other complaint or case exists. We have not reviewed the underlying 1993 civil or bankruptcy complaints or completed a case-by-case check of the named people.
